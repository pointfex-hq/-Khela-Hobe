How It Works

From Discovery to Kickoff in Four Steps

STEP 01

Discover Nearby

Filter Quick Matches by sport, Dhaka/Bangladesh area, skill tier, and Asia/Dhaka start time.

STEP 02

Join or Apply

Tap Join Match for Open Join games, or send a position note for Approval Required rosters.

STEP 03

Pledge Gear

Check remaining equipment needs and pledge what you can bring so nothing is missing at kickoff.

STEP 04

Show Up & Play

Meet at the public venue, check in with your organizer, and build your player reliability record.

Safety & Guardian Protection Principles

Designed to protect youth athletes and community members.

  • check_circle Public Venues Only: Match locations display verified turfs, courts, and fields—never private residential coordinates.
  • check_circle Authorized Guardian Linking: Safety IDs only initiate a linking request; child activity remains private until the child and backend approve authorization.
  • check_circle Active Moderation: Any player can report unsafe conduct or venue issues for rapid review by Khela Hobe moderators.

Contact & Community Support

Need help with a match dispute, venue listing, or guardian linking? Our Dhaka community team responds daily between 9:00 AM and 10:00 PM BST.
